# Astrology

The tropical zodiac divides longitude into equal 30° segments starting at the March equinox. Aspects compare the smaller angular separation with 0°, 60°, 90°, 120° and 180°. One configurable orb applies to all five aspects. A one-hour speed projection classifies applying and separating aspects, with 0.001° tolerance for exactness. Longitude speed determines direct or retrograde motion, with a near-stationary band of 0.001°/day. Exact aspect, station and ingress event detection are tracked separately.

A future sidereal provider must specify epoch, ayanamsa and convention. Lahiri, Fagan–Bradley and Raman use distinct zero-point conventions and can shift placements near sign boundaries. The baseline uses tropical consistently for positions and widget previews. Personal astrology should make house and angle outputs conditional on birth time and latitude. Traditional associations are presented as interpretations.
