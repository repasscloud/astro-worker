# Astrology

The tropical zodiac divides longitude into equal 30° segments starting at the March equinox. Aspects compare the smaller angular separation with 0°, 60°, 90°, 120° and 180°. One configurable orb applies to all five aspects. A one-hour speed projection classifies applying and separating aspects, with 0.001° tolerance for exactness. Longitude speed determines direct or retrograde motion, with a near-stationary band of 0.001°/day. The Planets detail dialog searches 183 days either side of the selected instant for the previous and next tropical ingress and direct and retrograde stations. The solver samples apparent geocentric motion daily, splits intervals at a reversal to catch both sign crossings, and refines each bracket to one second. That is solver resolution. The physical accuracy depends on the ephemeris. The March–April 2025 Mercury retrograde interval is checked against JPL Horizons hourly observer ecliptic longitudes (body 199, centre 500@399, quantity 31), allowing 30 minutes for the coarse reference. [Horizons API](https://ssd-api.jpl.nasa.gov/doc/horizons.html)

Exact aspect event detection is tracked separately.

A future sidereal provider must specify epoch, ayanamsa and convention. Lahiri, Fagan–Bradley and Raman use distinct zero-point conventions and can shift placements near sign boundaries. The baseline uses tropical consistently for positions and widget previews. Personal astrology should make house and angle outputs conditional on birth time and latitude. Traditional associations are presented as interpretations.
