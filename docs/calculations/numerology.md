# Numerology and editorial content

Reduce a value by adding its digits until it is a single digit, or retain 11, 22 and 33 when that setting is enabled. Reduce the civil year for the universal year, add the month and reduce for universal month, then add the day and reduce for universal day. For example, 2 February 2025 has universal year 9, universal month 11 and universal day 4 with master numbers enabled.

The personal year reduces the universal year plus reduced birth month and birth day. Add civil month and day at successive stages to find the personal month and day. The life path reduces year, month and day individually before reducing their sum. The birthday number is the reduced birth day. These are explicit choices among numerology conventions.

For a 29 February 2000 birth date, the life path is 6. For 2 February 2025, the personal year is 22 with master numbers enabled (9 + 2 + 11), and 4 with them disabled (9 + 2 + 2, then reduce 13). Both settings give personal month 6 and personal day 8. A leap-day birth date remains the original civil date, including in years without 29 February.

Version 2 of the bundled editorial catalog includes English and Spanish. Regional locales use their language edition, and unsupported languages or a missing translation use English. Universal day selects its colour, theme, affirmation, intention and interpretation, including entries for master numbers. A Gregorian civil-day index selects the reflection prompt. The interface labels remain in English. Editorial selections use the configured display date and run offline.
