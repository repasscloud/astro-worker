# ADR 0001 — Use a replaceable pure-Dart ephemeris for the foundation

Accepted for foundation development. Adopt Astronomia behind `AstronomyEngine` for one offline calculation path across the target platforms. Validate its results independently and preserve a provider boundary for future replacement or higher-accuracy resources. The owner subsequently approved [AGPL-3.0 distribution and Swiss Ephemeris evaluation](0003-agpl-distribution.md). Astronomia remains the foundation provider until a replacement is qualified. See the [ephemeris decision](../calculations/ephemeris.md).
